# NxtWave Campus Ambassador Toolkit

## Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes

**Campaign goal:** 500 registrations in 7 days
**Target audience:** Final-year engineering students
**Workshop:** Free, beginner-friendly, hands-on AI project session

---

## 1. What campus ambassadors do

Your goal is to help students discover a practical way to start building with AI.

1. Share the workshop with eligible students.
2. Explain what students can learn in 60 minutes.
3. Share your personal referral code with interested students.
4. Answer basic questions and direct students to the registration page.
5. Track registrations attributed to your referral code.
6. Share feedback about what students find interesting or confusing.

**Important:** Be transparent. Never promise certificates, jobs, placements, or workshop outcomes unless officially confirmed.

## 5. Five-minute campus outreach plan

- **Minute 1:** Identify a relevant class group, technical club, or student community.
- **Minute 2:** Share the short workshop message.
- **Minute 3:** Personally invite a few interested students.
- **Minute 4:** Answer questions honestly and share the registration link.
- **Minute 5:** Record the outreach activity and plan a follow-up.

Follow campus rules and group administrators' posting guidelines. Avoid spamming or repeatedly messaging students who aren't interested.
